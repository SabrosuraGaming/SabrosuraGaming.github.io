Privacy Policy

Sabrosura Gesture DJ, by Chahin Labs

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Sabrosura Gesture DJ is a desktop app that lets you mix two decks of music with hand gestures in front of your webcam. This page describes what the app does with information on your computer.

Camera

The app uses your webcam only to track your hands, and that processing happens entirely on your computer. The camera feed is not uploaded, recorded, or sent over the internet unless you start a session recording yourself (see below).

Optional local recording

Recording is off by default. If you start a session recording (the R key, or both thumbs up held for about a second), the app saves a video of what you see on screen (the camera plus the DJ overlay) and a WAV file of the mixed audio on your computer. By default these files go in your Videos\GestureDJ folder. They stay on your computer unless you share them yourself.

No account, no analytics

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not include analytics, advertising, or telemetry, and it does not contact the internet for those purposes.

Data stored on your computer

Settings, cue points, resume state, and a log file are stored locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\GestureDJ. You can delete that folder at any time to remove this data. Songs you load with the file picker are not copied there; the app just remembers paths and cues.
